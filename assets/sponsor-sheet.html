Why Sponsor
BSides Rocket City?

> SAT 2027-03-20// CALHOUN COMMUNITY COLLEGE// HUNTSVILLE, AL// FREE TO ATTEND
BSides Rocket City is Huntsville's free, volunteer-run information security conference — a day of technical talks, hands-on workshops, and villages where students, hackers, defenders, and rocket scientists learn from each other. Sponsors are how it stays free.
// why_sponsor
Keep it free. Every sponsor dollar removes a barrier between a curious student and their first security conference.
100% volunteer-run. Nobody is paid. Sponsorship goes directly into food, badges, and the event itself.
Invest where you hire. Support the pipeline of students and first-time speakers entering Huntsville's cyber and space-cyber workforce.
Community, not a trade show. On-site sponsor presence stays minimal and tasteful — your support speaks for itself.

The honest model: sponsors may be recognized on our website and select event materials, but sponsorship is community support — not influence. The community shows up, sponsors keep the lights on, and everyone leaves knowing more than they arrived with.
