About

BSides Rocket City is Huntsville's free, community-driven information security conference — by the community, for the community. Hosted in the heart of Rocket City, we bring together hackers, defenders, builders, researchers, students, and the merely curious for a day of technical talks, hands-on workshops, and the hallway conversations that make BSides what it is.

We're a volunteer-led conference designed to foster the next generation of defenders: a low-barrier platform where K-12 and university students collaborate directly with hackers, rocket scientists, researchers, and industry professionals.

From first exposure to advanced tradecraft, BSides Rocket City is built to connect curiosity with real-world experience. Free to attend. Bring your curiosity.

Call for Presenters

The CFP is open now and closes December 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM CT. We especially encourage students, first-time presenters, and new voices to submit.

15 min Lightning Talks

One sharp idea, tool, or lesson. Perfect for first-time speakers.

1 hr Talks

Room to go deep — research, demos, war stories, lessons learned.

multi-hr Workshops

Hands-on labs. Tell us what attendees will walk away able to do.

What we're looking for

Technical, hands-on, community-driven proposals across:

  • Ethical hacking — red, blue, and purple teaming
  • Space cyber, RF, hardware hacking, and lockpicking
  • Maker projects, AI security, cloud, and ICS/OT
  • Research, tools, demos, war stories, and lessons learned
  • Weird ideas worth sharing

…anything that helps people build, break, learn, and think differently.

What we're not accepting

No marketing pitches, no sales talks, no fluff, no business capability briefings. This is for real knowledge-sharing, technical depth, hands-on learning, and community-driven ideas. Everyone, including sponsors, submits through the same CFP.

Make your submission stand out

Lead with the problem and why the audience should care, then what you'll show. A clear outline with concrete, practical takeaways goes a long way. For workshops, tell us exactly what attendees will walk away able to do — and what they need to bring.
